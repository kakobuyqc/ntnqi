From discovery to decision

A practical Litbuy shopping guide.

Good Litbuy finds need more than an appealing photograph. This Litbuy spreadsheet guide follows a repeatable process: organize first, verify the listing, then compare the full purchase.

Start your category search ↗

  1. Choose one category and a purpose

    Decide whether you need everyday sneakers, a layering piece, a jersey, or a finishing accessory. A clear purpose makes a long product list easier to review.

  2. Record the source and exact variant

    Keep the original product URL, item name, seller, chosen color, and size together. Litbuy product links should be checked at the source; a saved link can lead to a changed or unavailable listing.

  3. Check measurements and available photos

    Read the size chart and material description. When quality-control photos are available, compare the selected variant, construction, and visible condition. Photos alone do not prove authenticity.

  4. Compare the full cost before paying

    Account for the item, applicable delivery charges, service fees, and destination taxes or duties. Confirm your chosen service accepts the product link and review its current purchase terms.

Useful answers, upfront

Before you follow a product link.

A short reference for readers using a spreadsheet to compare products, plan an order, or organize new finds.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product-discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Useful entries record the source listing, category, selected size, measurements, price, and any available quality-control photos. This page is an independent guide, not an official Litbuy spreadsheet.

How do I use Litbuy product links?

Open a source listing, check the selected item and variant, and confirm whether your chosen shopping service accepts that link before paying. The buttons on this page open CAFANS browsing pages; they do not import products into a Litbuy account.

Does a spreadsheet verify authenticity or product quality?

No. A list, brand name, or reference image does not verify authenticity, seller reliability, or product quality. Review the actual seller, item details, photographs, and applicable buyer protections.

What should I check before choosing a clothing size?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item you own. Check chest width, length, shoulder width, sleeve length, waist, or inseam as appropriate. Confirm the measurement units and whether the figures describe body size or garment size.

How should I estimate the total order cost?

Record the item price and currency, then add any applicable domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes, and duties. Treat shipping estimates as provisional until package details and the destination are confirmed.

No. This is an independent shopping reference linking to CAFANS. It is not operated by Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, or Hermès.
